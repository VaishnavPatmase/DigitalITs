Capabilities

Relocations, without the operational drama.

IT moves are easy to underestimate and expensive to botch. We bring the project discipline, technical depth, and physical logistics that make them predictable.

Office IT Decommissioning

Workstation, printer, peripheral, and server room teardown. Asset tagging, data wipe, and packing for transport.

Structured Cabling & Installation

Cat6, Cat6A, fibre backbone, patch panel design, and certified test results to current standards.

Server Room & MDF Relocation

Rack-and-stack, UPS, cooling, and environmental monitoring. Designed for the new site, validated before cutover.

Workstation Re-commissioning

Re-imaging, asset re-tagging, user-ready setup, and white-glove desk delivery where required.

Network Cutover & Testing

Phased cutover plans, rollback procedures, and pre/post testing for every circuit and service.

Project & Change Management

Single point of contact, detailed Gantt, weekly steering updates, and on-site supervision through go-live.

Engagement flow
