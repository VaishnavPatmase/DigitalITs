Capabilities

Boardrooms that match the work.

We design and deliver complete meeting-room environments from acoustic treatment and lighting through to control systems, displays, and certified cabling.

Acoustic Treatment & Design

Sound-absorbing panels, ceiling clouds, and HVAC noise mitigation. Engineered to STC and RT60 standards.

Display & Projection Systems

4K displays, video walls, projection, and direct-view LED. Sized and specified for the room, not the catalogue.

Camera, Mic & Speaker Systems

Beam-forming mic arrays, auto-tracking cameras, and DSP-tuned speakers. So every voice and every face is captured cleanly.

Lighting Design & Control

Tunable white, scene presets, and integration with room control. The lighting that makes video calls actually look professional.

Control Systems & Automation

Crestron, Extron, Q-SYS, and custom control. One-touch room startup, source switching, and shutdown.

Certified Cabling & Infrastructure

Cat6A, fibre backbone, certified test reports, and as-built documentation. The boring stuff that makes everything else work.
