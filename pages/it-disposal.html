Capabilities

Disposal that protects data, brand, and planet.

Every retired asset is treated as a live data risk until it's proven otherwise. We provide the proof, and the documentation your auditors will accept.

On-Site Data Destruction

Hard drive sanitisation and shredding at your site. Witnessed, logged, and certified before anything leaves the building.

NIST 800-88 Compliant Sanitisation

Purge and clear protocols aligned to NIST 800-88 Rev 1. Tools include Blancco, BitRaser, and hardware secure-erase where supported.

ADISA-Accredited Processing

Asset processing through ADISA-accredited partners. International standards for IT asset sanitisation.

Asset Inventory & Valuation

Pre-disposal audits, residual value assessment, redeployment, and resale where appropriate. We maximise recovery before recycling.

Certificate of Destruction

Per-asset certificates, serial-number matched, available as PDF or via secure portal. Audit-ready in 24 hours.

Environmental & ESG Reporting

Weight-based diversion reporting, downstream vendor audits, and ESG metrics ready for your sustainability report.

Engagement flow

From active asset to certified retirement.

01

Inventory

Complete asset register with serial numbers, locations, and data classification. The single source of truth.

02

03

Process

Sanitisation, destruction, and downstream processing under audited controls.

04

Report

Per-asset certificates, ESG metrics, and asset recovery revenue (where applicable).
