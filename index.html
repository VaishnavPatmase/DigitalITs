How we engage

A four-step path from intent to outcome.

Repeatable, opinionated, calibrated for speed and risk. No surprise invoices, no "phase 17" hand-offs.

01

Discover

Two-week diagnostic. We map the system, surface the gaps, and put a number on the upside. You get a written brief, not a slide deck.

02

Design

Architecture, success metrics, and a fixed-scope plan. We agree the definition of done before any line is shipped.

03

Build

Two-week sprints, weekly demos, shared Slack channels. The work is always live in a real environment never slides.

04

Operate

We hand over, train your team, and stay on call. Or we run it for you. Either way, you own the system end to end.
